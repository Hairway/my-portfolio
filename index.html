My Workflow

How I Work

From initial concept to shipped playable — a hands-on process built around strong gameplay, clear UX, and smooth production.

01
Ideation & Research
Market research, competitor analysis, and gameplay hook exploration. I define the core idea and shape the playable around mechanics that are easy to understand and engaging to interact with.
02
Spec Writing
Detailed GDDs covering mechanics, tutorial flow, UI, pacing, and end-card logic. Clear documentation that keeps production efficient and aligned across the team.
03
Team Briefing
Close collaboration with developers, artists, and QA throughout production. I stay hands-on during the full production cycle to keep the playable polished and consistent.
04
Launch & Iterate
Post-launch iteration and creative improvements based on performance feedback and player behavior. Insights from live results help refine future concepts and mechanics.

About Me

Dima Fomin
Playable Ads Producer & Strategist

I'm a Creative Producer specializing in playable ads, gameplay-driven concepts, and interactive mobile creatives.

My work covers the full playable production cycle — from gameplay hooks and concept development to specs, team coordination, iteration, and shipped creatives.

Before focusing on playables, I worked in advertising agencies producing creative campaigns and commercial shoots. That background shaped my approach to playable ads: combining strong visual communication with intuitive gameplay and performance-focused design.

The playables I created and led through production later became internal creative benchmarks and starting points for future iterations.
