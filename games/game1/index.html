Kingdom Clash

Concept · Spec · Full production

Let the player buy squads — but not endlessly. Money only comes back after the army has been sent into battle, so every round is a closed loop of spend, fight, earn, spend more. Three rounds, each against a bigger red army than the last.

Location
  • The blue kingdom's castle sits along the bottom of the screen, with forest and bushes across the top.
  • Enemy waves come out of those bushes and walk down towards the castle.
  • A 3×5 grid appears under the blue army — that grid is where everything the player does happens.
  • Between rounds the field disappears entirely and the two armies clash on open ground, which keeps the fight and the planning visually separate.
Scenes
Scene 1
Opening
  • A small blue army — one swordsman, one archer, one spearman, all level 1 — is standing in front of the castle.
  • The camera pushes in on them, then the red army appears out of the bushes at the top: four level 1 swordsmen.
  • The camera then drops down to the playfield, which is where the player will act.
  • PLAY NOW is active from the very first frame.
Scene 2
Buy and merge
  • The 3×5 grid appears under the blue army's feet and three purchase buttons come up: swordsman, archer, spearman.
  • Every unit costs 10 to start, and the coin bar opens at 30 — so the player can afford exactly three units before the money is gone.
  • The price rises by 10 only on the button that was tapped. A player who never buys spearmen still sees 10 on that button at the end of the playable.
  • Bought units appear on a free cell of the grid; ideally they run in from the bottom of the screen as if coming out of the castle.
  • A pulsing UPGRADE YOUR ARMY plate sits over the field while there is money left to spend.
  • Once two identical units are on the grid, the finger drags one onto the other and they become a single level 2 squad — the icon changes from a 1 to a 2.
Tutorial · finger jumps between the buy buttons, then shows the merge — MERGE THE SOLDIERS
Scene 3
First battle
  • When the money runs out the TO BATTLE button appears.
  • The grid disappears and the two armies walk into each other on open ground.
  • Swordsmen take melee, archers shoot from behind them, spearmen deal medium damage at short range.
  • Fallen soldiers disappear from the field rather than piling up.
  • The blues win and coins fly out of the dead reds into the coin bar — 150 for this round.
  • The surviving squad walks back and the grid returns. Losses are replenished automatically: units of the same level as the ones that died run in from the bottom.
Scene 4
Rounds 2 and 3
  • The second red wave is three swordsmen, three archers and two spearmen — visibly denser than the first.
  • The player spends the 150 on the same three buttons, at whatever prices their own buying pattern has produced.
  • Winning round two pays 300, so the budget roughly doubles each time while the enemy grows faster than that.
  • The third wave is a deliberately large army that reads as stronger than anything the player has assembled, which is what forces the last round of merges.
  • If a purchase is unaffordable the button goes black and white rather than disappearing, so the player can still see what it would cost.
  • Five to seven seconds of inactivity replays the tutorial on the next useful action.
End states
Win
Third wave held
  • Condition: the player survives to the end of the third wave.
  • If they still have money left, the tutorial finger points at the buy buttons before the fight starts.
  • PLAY NOW in the centre; any tap on the screen goes to the store.
Fail
Castle taken
  • Condition: the player spends everything and still loses the round.
  • The reds run at the castle, the camera cuts to it in close-up and a red vignette closes in.
  • The blue flags on the towers turn red.
  • DEFEAT, then a pulsing TRY NOW.
