Pocket Tales

Direction · Brief · Mechanics

Pull fresh mechanics out of the game itself instead of restating the old ones: one rescue-and-rebuild funnel that runs drag, tap, upgrade and build in a single sitting, and closes on the same bridge it opened with.

Location
  • Summer island from the game, cut by a river, with the bridge across it as the centre of the scene.
  • The settlement foundation sits on the near bank — a floor with stakes rather than a finished building, so the upgrade has somewhere visible to land.
  • Wolves hold the far bank once the bridge is down, which keeps the threat on screen without it ever reaching the player.
Scenes
Scene 1
Opening
  • Auto-play, no input asked for. The camera shows three villagers running down the screen in panic with ! icons over their heads.
  • The camera lifts and reveals the wolves chasing them, with red danger icons above the pack.
  • The villagers reach the bridge and run onto it.
  • The bridge breaks apart in pieces under them and they fall into the river.
  • The wolves stop at the bank and wait — they never cross, so the pressure stays constant instead of escalating.
  • The villagers swim in place, reaching out of the water, which is the state the player is about to act on.
Scene 2
Pull them out
  • A translucent green platform on the foundation marks the drop zone and shimmers so it reads as a target.
  • A villager lifts as if picked up by the scruff and hangs in the air before moving.
  • He then follows the finger with a soft shadow under him, so the drag has weight.
  • Released over the green zone he lands with a puff of smoke — a cloud that resolves into the villager.
  • Released near the wolves he is lost, with a lunge, a bite and a cry. The fail state is reachable by the player's own hand.
Tutorial · hand drags the first villager from the water to the green zone
Scene 3
Chop
  • A white bubble with a wood icon sits over the tree and clears on the first tap, so the target is unmistakable once and then never again.
  • The tree falls through three states — full with foliage, bare trunk, stump.
  • Logs drop beside it and then fly into the counter at the top right.
  • The counter is what gates the upgrade, so the chopping has a visible destination.
Tutorial · hand taps the tree
Scene 4
Upgrade
  • The building panel opens with the bow upgrade on it and an UPGRADE button.
  • The villagers come out carrying bows — the upgrade is visible on the characters, not only in the UI.
  • Sparkle particles play around the building on completion.
Tutorial · hand taps UPGRADE
Scene 5
Build the bridge
  • A hammer bubble appears over the broken bridge.
  • Tapping rebuilds it, with a magic sting on the new span.
  • It is the same bridge that collapsed in the opening, which closes the loop the playable started with — the player fixes the thing they watched break.
Tutorial · hand taps the hammer bubble
End states
Win
Bridge rebuilt
  • The settlement is safe, the route is open, and the playable goes to the endcard and the CTA.
Fail
Villagers lost
  • Large red FAIL, the game logo, and a green RETRY button.
